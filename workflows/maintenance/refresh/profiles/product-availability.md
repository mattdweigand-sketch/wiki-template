# Product Availability Profile

Use for generally available, beta, preview, waitlist, deprecated, plan-limited, region-limited, platform-limited, and prerequisite claims.

## Evidence Priority

1. Current official product documentation, release notes, and administration guidance.
2. Current product owner pages whose authority references those sources.
3. Dated internal launch guidance with explicit audience and rollout scope.
4. Announcements and sales material as evidence of a dated statement, not continuing availability.

## Owner

Use the narrowest existing `wiki/products/` or `wiki/features/` page that owns the capability. A broad product page should not absorb unrelated availability states merely to avoid another owner.

## Claim Boundaries

- Record status with an as-of date plus region, platform, plan, tenant, and rollout cohort when applicable.
- Separate announced, enabled, and contractually available.
- Do not infer general availability from one customer instance, screenshot, demo, or roadmap statement.
- Preserve deprecation notices and migration paths when retiring old availability language.

## Review Backstop

Recommend review within 14 days for active launches and 30 days for established availability claims.

Review intervals are advisory and may be adapted to the domain. They do not schedule an automation.
