# Pricing And Entitlements Profile

Use for list prices, negotiated commercial terms, credits, included usage, overages, plan names, packaging, and entitlement boundaries.

## Evidence Priority

1. Current signed order forms or authoritative account-specific terms for that account and date.
2. Current official pricing, billing, and entitlement documentation for general claims.
3. Dated internal commercial guidance with explicit scope.
4. Older decks and sales notes only as historical evidence.

## Owner

Find the existing product, policy, or concept page that owns the relevant pricing and entitlements for this domain. Enroll the actual replacement owner in `scripts/current-state-owners.json` before retiring a claim.

## Claim Boundaries

- Keep currency, billing interval, plan, geography, customer scope, effective date, and taxes or discounts attached to the claim.
- Never generalize one negotiated order form into public list pricing.
- Treat a changed plan name separately from a changed entitlement.
- If two current authorities disagree, record the contradiction and do not choose the more convenient number.

## Review Backstop

Recommend review within 30 days, and immediately after packaging, credit, or billing changes.

Review intervals are advisory and may be adapted to the domain. They do not schedule an automation.
