# Talk Track Profile

Use for the core company or product narrative, value proposition, approved vocabulary, differentiation, and the short language sellers or agents are expected to reuse.

## Evidence Priority

1. Current official product and enterprise guidance named by the relevant owner page.
2. Recent, attributable internal product or go-to-market direction preserved through ingest.
3. Current product owner pages and supported feature/system pages.
4. Historical sales collateral and call transcripts as evidence of what was said, not proof that it remains approved or true.

## Owner

Search first for an existing current-state page that can own the affected narrative. If no owner fits, propose a page in the appropriate existing entity type. An analysis is appropriate only when it meets the [analysis capture qualifications](../../../research/ask.md#analysis-capture); creating it requires the existing exact approval gate.

## Claim Boundaries

- Separate durable positioning from volatile feature, availability, price, security, and retention claims. Route those claims through their dedicated profiles.
- Retire wording only when newer evidence or an explicit messaging decision supersedes it. A stylistic preference alone is a page edit, not a banned claim.
- Preserve attributed historical pitches in source pages.
- Do not turn aspirational language, a customer quote, or a draft deck into verified product reality.

## Review Backstop

Recommend another review within 30 days for the canonical talk-track owner, or sooner after a material product or positioning change. The date is a review prompt, not evidence that the content stayed current.

Review intervals are advisory and may be adapted to the domain. They do not schedule an automation.
