# Security And Retention Profile

Use for data use, model training, retention, deletion, encryption, access, certifications, subprocessors, residency, and compliance statements.

## Evidence Priority

1. Current signed contractual terms for the named customer and scope.
2. Current official legal, privacy, security, Trust Center, and product administration documentation.
3. Current security and policy owner pages that point to those authorities.
4. Dated internal guidance and customer discussions only within their stated scope.

## Owner

Find the existing policy, system, product, or process page that governs the claim in this domain. Enroll the exact replacement owner, not a convenient neighboring page.

## Claim Boundaries

- Keep product, plan, tenant setting, data class, jurisdiction, contract, and effective date attached to the claim.
- Distinguish retention from training, logging, backup persistence, and deletion timing.
- Do not convert a security control, certification, roadmap item, or sales assurance into a broader legal promise.
- Conflicting contract and public-policy claims remain explicit until qualified review resolves their scope. Attorney or security review remains the final step for consequential use.

## Review Backstop

Recommend review within 30 days and immediately after a legal, policy, Trust Center, or contractual change.

Review intervals are advisory and may be adapted to the domain. They do not schedule an automation.
